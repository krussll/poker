### Responsible Gaming Support
River Pairs is an informational and statistical reference resource. If you or someone you know is seeking assistance with problem gambling, please contact the free national support helplines:
- **UK**: [BeGambleAware.org](https://www.begambleaware.org) (0808 8020 133)
- **US**: [National Council on Problem Gambling](https://www.ncpgambling.org) (1-800-522-4700)
- **International**: [Gambling Therapy](https://www.gamblingtherapy.org)
