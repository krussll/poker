## Texas Hold'em Pocket Pairs: Complete Odds & Matchup Directory

In Texas Hold'em, receiving a pocket pair is one of the most exciting pre-flop moments. A pocket pair occurs on average once every **17 hands** (approximately **5.88%** of deals).

However, not all pocket pairs are created equal. This guide organizes pre-flop odds, set-mining probabilities, and key heads-up confrontations across all 13 pocket pairs.

---

## 1. Pocket Pair Hierarchy & Pre-Flop Categories

| Tier | Pairs | Relative Strength | Primary Strategic Role |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Monster Overpairs** | **AA, KK** | Elite favorites (~80%+ equity vs non-pairs) | Raise, 3-bet, and stack-off pre-flop. |
| **Premium High Pairs** | **QQ, JJ, TT** | Very strong (~70–80% vs underpairs, ~54% vs overcards) | Value open; play cautiously against heavy pre-flop 4-bets. |
| **Middle Pairs** | **99, 88, 77** | Solid (~50–54% coin flips vs two overcards) | Isolate weak limpers; call raises with position and implied odds. |
| **Low / Small Pairs** | **66, 55, 44, 33, 22** | Speculative (~52% vs 2 overcards, ~18% vs higher pairs) | "Set mining": call cheaply to flop three-of-a-kind. |

## 2. Key Mathematical Probabilities for Pocket Pairs

- **Chance of flopping a set (three-of-a-kind)**: **11.76%** (approx. 1 in 8.5 times).
- **Chance of flopping a full house**: **0.73%** (approx. 1 in 137 times).
- **Chance of flopping four-of-a-kind (quads)**: **0.24%** (approx. 1 in 416 times).
- **Overcard on the flop for Pocket Kings (KK)**: **22.5%** (an Ace appears on the flop ~1 in 4.4 times).
- **Overcard on the flop for Pocket Queens (QQ)**: **41.4%** (an Ace or King appears on the flop).
- **Overcard on the flop for Pocket Jacks (JJ)**: **57.4%** (an Ace, King, or Queen appears).
